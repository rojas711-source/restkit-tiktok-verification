RestKit TikTok Uploader Privacy

The RestKit TikTok Uploader uses TikTok authorization only to let the store owner upload approved RestKit videos as TikTok drafts. It does not send Shopify customer order data to TikTok.

The uploader may process the selected video file, caption text, TikTok account authorization response, and related upload status. TikTok account access tokens are stored server-side only when the integration is enabled.

RestKit shopper privacy information is available at RestKit Privacy Policy.
